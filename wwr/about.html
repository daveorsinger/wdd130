History

Rapid River Rafting was created with a passion for adventure in the outdoors. Over the years, we have helped families and groups create memories to last a lifetime of exciting white water rafting trips.

Rapid River Rafting continues to provide exciting white water rafting trips for families, friends, and groups of all sizes looking to have adventures on the river.
